Internal pipe climber: building and testing a prototype

Goal
Build a wheel-driven robot that travels inside a vertical pipe and responds to target markings.
My work

Iteration

02 / Undergraduate final project · Report: April 2026

Comparing NACA aerofoils for small UAV applications

Goal
Compare NACA 2412 and NACA 4412 for low-Reynolds-number UAV applications.
My work
I compared aerodynamic coefficients using XFOIL and selected ANSYS Fluent cases, interpreted flow fields, and evaluated the modelling limits.
Finding
At the selected condition, NACA 4412 produced higher lift and drag coefficients than NACA 2412.
Judgement
I weighed lift against drag under matched operating conditions and considered the two-dimensional model’s limits before drawing design conclusions.
Methods, source values & flow-field images

The main XFOIL comparison used Re = 2 × 105 and 3 × 105. Complementary Fluent cases used Re = 1 × 105, 2 × 105 and 3 × 105, at 0° and 8°. The Fluent model used SST k–ω without a transition model.

This is a two-dimensional numerical comparison, without wind-tunnel or flight testing. The selected case does not establish an overall optimum or full-aircraft performance.

03 / Team course project · February–May 2024

Ball-throwing vehicle

Goal
Develop a mobile vehicle for ball pickup and throwing into a basket.
My work
I took the main programming role and contributed to the team’s design and assembly.
Design
A gear-driven pivoting arm with adjustable swing, mounted on a four-wheeled chassis.
Judgement
My design uses one arm for pickup and throwing, so its travel must accommodate both actions. The adjustable swing was intended to help aiming.
Output
A multi-view CAD drawing and an individual report documenting the mechanism and my contribution.
